## Added attributes seen on the most sites
- body background color (color, 4 sites): AI-built pages most commonly use an off-white body background (2 of 4 sites, the others near-black and pale grey-blue), whereas originals lean darker or neutral (2 near-black, 1 pale grey, 1 white).
- color palette (color, 4 sites): Each AI-built site has its own distinct palette with no dominant value (one even matching the original's red, blue and charcoal), while originals are likewise each different, typically a neutral or white base with one or two accents.
- promotional and call-out sections (component, 4 sites): AI-built pages use CSS-built, rounded panels, tiles and chips, and often omit elements such as announcement bars, search bars and press bands, while originals rely on raster images, text links and bars.
- copy tone (copy, 4 sites): All 4 AI-built pages use plain sentences (and the one page checked for emoji has none), while the originals vary across plain sentences, user-generated thread titles, exclamatory CTAs and lowercase quirky copy with emoji, with only one original being plain and the one checked original also lacking emoji.
- visual effects usage (decoration, 4 sites): AI-built pages typically use few or no effects (three of four sites have none, no gradients or only a few transitions, with one using gradients, blur and transitions, and one using header-only backdrop blur), whereas originals more often combine shadows, gradients and many transitions or animations.
- corner radius usage (decoration, 4 sites): Most AI-built pages (3 of 4) use small rounded corners and one uses pills and large radii, whereas all four originals are mostly square.
- main content layout (layout, 4 sites): AI-built pages are mostly stacked full-width sections (4 of 5 observations) with one centered column, while the originals mostly use a two-column layout with a sidebar (4 of 5) and one unstyled stack.
- page length (layout, 4 sites): AI-built pages are split evenly between short (about 2200–3950px) and long (about 5100–5650px), while three originals are short and one is very long at about 29,000px.
- dom weight (semantic_dom, 4 sites): All AI-built pages have a minimal DOM, whereas all originals are heavy, either div-based (3) or plugin-laden (2).
- heading hierarchy (semantic_dom, 4 sites): All AI-built pages use a clean h1, section h2s and h3s hierarchy, whereas all originals have flat or irregular headings, often with an empty h1.
- image alt text quality (semantic_dom, 4 sites): Most AI-built pages (3 of 4) have complete descriptive alts, while the originals mostly have missing or wrong alts (3 of 4); one site in each set has empty alts on editorial images.
- page metadata richness (semantic_dom, 4 sites): All AI-built pages carry only minimal basic metadata, whereas all originals have rich Open Graph and JSON-LD metadata.
- semantic landmarks (semantic_dom, 4 sites): AI-built pages most often use a single header, nav and footer (2 of 4), while originals most often use many headers, sections and footers (2 of 4), with other patterns varying on both sides.
- body line height (typography, 4 sites): AI-built pages all use 1.6–1.65 line height (split evenly across 4 sites), while originals mostly use 1.5 (3 of 4) with one at 2.0.
- social links style (component, 3 sites): AI-built social elements use pills, text links and circular icons (and text-only tiles where originals had broken images), versus the originals' icon glyphs, square tiles and strips.
