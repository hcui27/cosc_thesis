5 Questions, 5 Answers About Thync

1 What does Thync do?

We build devices usable by everyone that improve brain function via electricity, the natural language of the brain.

We were founded in 2010, and have since researched and developed how to democratize access to the benefits of neurostimulation for everyone.

2 How does Thync improve brain function with electricity?

For 10+ years, we've developed and tested various non-invasive neurostimulation devices, including headsets and patches.

These wearable devices send electric stimulation programs we've designed to specific areas of the brain and peripheral nervous system we've selected to instigate the desired positive effect.

3 Why do you believe that non-invasive brain stimulation works?

Two reasons: 1) we cross-referenced our work with the broader literature from the greater scientific community, and 2) we've tested various iterations of our products for over ten years, not just in research labs, but with reputable partner institutions, influential minds, and, of course, on ourselves.

4 How are you validating your technology?

Our product research team constantly runs trials to test the different effects of electric stimulation on health, behavior, and physiology.

Thync has also partnered with academic labs and organizations around the world to test our products with unique populations, including athletes, gamers, military, and surgeons.

5 How can I try Thync’s technology?

Please visit our website feelzing.com to learn about a wearable patch that increases energy, clarity, and alertness for a few hours — think 5-hour energy, neurostimulation style.
