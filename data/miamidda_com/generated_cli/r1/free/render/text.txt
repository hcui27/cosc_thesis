WHO WE ARE

Growing the economic health and vitality of Downtown Miami

The Miami Downtown Development Authority (Miami DDA) is an independent agency of the City of Miami, funded by a special tax levy on properties inside its district boundaries.

As an autonomous city agency, it advocates, facilitates, plans and carries out business development, planning, capital improvements, and marketing and communications strategies.

Governance

A 15-member board of directors, made up of three public appointees and 12 downtown property owners, business owners and/or residents, sets policy direction. A multi-disciplinary staff team under the executive director implements it.

The Authority is committed to carrying out its mission collaboratively, ethically and professionally, in line with its public purpose.
