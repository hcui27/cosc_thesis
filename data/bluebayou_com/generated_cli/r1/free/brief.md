## Organisation

## Opening announcement
Opening Day: May 29, 2021. Because of recent weather and the forecast for the following week, Blue Bayou Water Park is pushing back its opening to May 29, 2021 at 10:00 a.m. The park hopes to welcome guests, their families and friends during the summer.

## Return-visit policy
Guests who pay the full daily admission price and have their ticket validated before leaving that day can come back the following day for free, as long as that second day is not a Saturday.
