Tasting Experiences

With more than forty wines produced on-site in our state-of-the-art winemaking facility, a restaurant serving up cuisine made to pair perfectly with our wines, all on a property surrounded by sustainably farmed vineyards, we are proud to offer a variety of Wine Experiences hosted by our knowledgeable and entertaining hospitality team.

Learn More
02

Pools & Park

Situated in the center of the park area at the winery, two swimming pools totaling 3,600 square feet are surrounded by chaise lounge chairs, which have become a welcome addition for children and adults on warm Alexander Valley days.

Learn More
03

Rustic Restaurant

Francis Ford Coppola shares his personal pantheon of recipes at the winery’s full-service restaurant. Set in a Sonoma County-casual atmosphere, the menu embraces international as well as traditional Italian dishes.
