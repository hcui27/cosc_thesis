Unlocking greater human potential.

In every city, there’s greater human potential waiting to be unlocked. We find it in the inconsistencies between bike routes and rail stops—and the gaps between bus routes and dining districts. With multiple transportation platforms, we complement existing transit systems to unlock it.

It’s nice to move you—today.

We’re a turnkey, full-service operation for municipalities, corporations, hospitals and universities, ready to deploy the right-size fleet of self-driving, attendant-monitored shuttles in your city.

Designing routes that matter.

Long before we deliver your fleet of shuttles, we study your city’s traffic and commuter flow, identifying bottlenecks and gaps in the current public transit schema to design the most optimal routes for your communities.

Green is our favorite color.

With our service, reduced congestion and emissions become a measurable metric. But that’s not the only positive impact. The data we collect will deliver valuable insights into the efficiency and sustainability of your public transportation services as a whole. Simply said, we provide the insights you need to transform.

Rider safety is job one.

Redundant sensor technology and our multi-policy decision-making system (MPDM) work together to not just see the road and react quickly, but to anticipate potential hazards—simulating 1000s of possibilities in the time it took to read this paragraph.

Closing the “last mile” gap for commuters.

Want to park in the cheaper lots but don’t want to make that long walk? If we’re in your city, it’s likely we’ve filled that gap with a convenient route to get you there. Hop in. It’s nice to move you!

Clean, safe, and sanitized.

UV-C lights that disinfect our shuttles after every rider. Upgraded HVAC filtration to increase air changeover. Onboard hand sanitizer stations. A partition between fleet attendant and riders. And disinfectant fogging between shuttle shifts. Green and squeaky clean.

Wait. Is that a driver?

While our shuttles are self-driving vehicles, each one is piloted by one of our friendly fleet attendants to ensure all systems are a go. And of course, they’re always ready to greet you with a smile and answer any questions you may have along the way.

WE GET AROUND

Check out some of the past and current communities we’ve had the pleasure of serving.

For more information, visit Arlington's RAPID website.
May's first international route brings autonomous mobility to the University of Hiroshima campus in Higashihiroshima, Japan.
We're bringing the future of mobility to Indiana with two unique autonomous vehicle services in the cities of Indianapolis and Fishers.
Currently the longest public autonomous, fixed route in Michigan. 3.2 miles, 20 stops, and more than 70,000 rides to-date.

For more information about the route, visit the AVGR website.
From December 2018 to September 2019, our shuttles autonomously drove over 19,000 miles in the most populous city in the great state of Ohio.
