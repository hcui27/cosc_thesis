OUR ETHOS

By partnering with experienced local designers, manufacturers and fabric suppliers, we are creating a fashion brand that puts customers first and offers slow fashion at an affordable price. We don’t cut corners and we don’t look for the easy way out — a difficult scenario is one we see as exciting and the solution will always provide an exciting product to our customers.

As a proud member of 1% for the Planet, we give a portion of every sale to support ocean cleanups and marine conservation.

About Bather

Launched in 2013, we set out to create the perfect poolside compliment to a well-curated closet of essentials — quality surf and swim products with a street-inspired approach that can be worn on or off the beach.

Bather is also a proud member of 1% for the Planet. Every purchase supports ocean cleanups and marine conservation.
