Main Content

The Frangipani Creative is the studio of Corinne Rodrigues.

A lover of words. A self-acceptance blogger. A book reviewer. A woman happily journeying through midlife, moving from self-improvement to self-acceptance and enjoying being herself.
I write about life, wellness, relationships at Everyday Gyaan. An avid reader, I review books at CorinneRodrigues.com and offer coaching to anyone looking to explore their creativity at The Frangipani Creative, located in Secunderabad, India.

Stay tuned for details on programs, courses, training and other offline events.
