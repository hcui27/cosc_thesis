Welcome to Nevada’s Department of Tourism and Cultural Affairs

The Department of Tourism and Cultural Affairs is a cabinet level agency that works to market Nevada as a premier destination for leisure and business travelers and enhance the quality of life for all who live in the state.

On this site, you’ll find a wealth of Nevada’s most exciting, intriguing, creative and historic experiences. We hope you’ll explore the myriad places and meet the fascinating people who make living in and visiting the state so extraordinary. Follow the links in the tabs above to discover Nevada’s remarkable tourism and cultural arts offerings.

For our partners in Nevada tourism, arts, history, museum and Indian affairs, the site also includes resources to enhancing your marketing and operational efforts.
