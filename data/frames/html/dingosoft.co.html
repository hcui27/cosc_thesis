Why choose Dingosoft ?
Over 20 years Accpac / Sage 300 SDK development experience
Our Sage 300 development experience goes back to Version 1 of "CA-Accpac 2000" as it was known then and our reputation is second to none. In fact, when Sage needed a product developed here in Australia they chose Dingosoft to do the development work for them.
Awesome support when you need it - which isn't often
One of the benefits of developing all our products in the Sage 300 SDK is that compared to external products, there is very little support required because generally if Sage 300 is working, then our products are working. However, when support is required, all our support is handled promptly by a former Sage 300 national support manager.
Easy online ordering with instant electronic delivery

You can then simply enter a purchase order and get instant electronic delivery of a serial number and activation code to turn the 30-day demo into a live product. Our customer portal keeps track of all your clients products and automatically handles the annual maintenance.
Products that are best in class
Our philosophy is simple: have the best product in the market and people will buy it. All of our effort goes into development and we have no salespeople and do very little marketing. We only want products that sell themselves.
