Who we are.

We are a government-funded non-for-profit mental health service. Our team of experienced mental health professionals, including clinical psychologists, psychiatrists, and mental health nurses, have supported more than 150,000 Australians. Our treatment courses are effective and backed by clinical trials from the eCentreClinic – a leading specialist research clinic at Macquarie University.
