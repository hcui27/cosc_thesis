Anna

Her digital marketing background and strategic execution pack a punch that powers social campaigns as they break through the noise and deliver results.

“I have the opportunity to work with some of the most talented, driven, creative individuals - it’s energizing to be a part of such a remarkable community and culture.”

Project & Products Manager

As a research maven with experience in market research and product management, skilled in survey design and optimization, marketing, product placement and e-commerce, Mak loves being challenged with new projects, creating new products, and engaging with her team.

Creative Director

“I enjoy ‘getting in the zone’ when I am challenged to create new concepts, rethink approaches and collaborate with team members who have deep understanding about the markets they serve.”

Claudine’s inherent understanding of all of the unique skill sets and job types that make up Endeavor’s diverse team of professionals shines a light on her commitment and dedication. “Everyone strives to provide the highest quality products in their individual fields of expertise and it cultivates an amazing spirit of collaboration and trust throughout the organization.”
Kelsey
Account Manager

Kelsey is a powerful partner for her clients, collaborating with them on marketing strategies and developing innovative programs to help them achieve their goals. Part consultant, marketer, storyteller and product expert, Kelsey brings it all together for her customers so they can deliver dynamic, multi-channel campaigns that succeed!

“Working with team members that are so dedicated to our markets, audiences and clients is uplifting, I feel so fortunate to have such a strong, supportive team invested in helping our clients meet their goals.”
Curt
Vice President, Production Operations

Having been involved with media production operations since the 80's, Curt has experienced first-hand the remarkable transformation of production tools and processes. In his role, Curt oversees Endeavor production workflows, scheduling, expense forecasting and management of vendor partnerships.

“The interaction I have with so many aspects of our business: sales, art, editorial, marketing, audience development, customers and vendors really helps me understand and value everyone’s contribution.”
