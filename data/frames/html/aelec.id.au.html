ABOUT AELEC

AELEC is a local, professional, family run company. We pride ourselves on valuing our clients needs while providing a quality and reliable service for all Electrical, Refrigeration, Solar Installation and Air Conditioning.

Our professional tradespeople support other local businesses and staff to further build a strong foundation for the growth of our community. We believe in quality workmanship and excellent value for money service.

AELEC tradespeople are highly qualified and professional with many years of industry experience, ranging from heavy industrial, agricultural, small urban business and domestic situations.

We are happy to answer any questions or address any concerns about all aspects electrical!
