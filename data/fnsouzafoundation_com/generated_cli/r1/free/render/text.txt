HISTORY & WORKS

Modern & Contemporary Art: Francis Newton Souza

Souza was a trailblazer and the first Indian artist to gain worldwide recognition. Western critics called him “India’s mystical expressionist.” His flowing, graphic manner and his provocative distortions of the human body produce an emotional response that changes how people conventionally see things.
