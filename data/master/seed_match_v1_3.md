## Added attributes seen on the most sites
- color palette (color, 4 sites): Most AI-built pages changed the overall palette to a different scheme, such as navy and cyan, monochrome green or cream with burnt orange, while one kept the original brand palette.
- copy tone (copy, 4 sites): On most sites the AI-built pages rewrite the original's casual, exclamatory, emoji-laden or user-generated copy into short, plain, sentence-case sentences, and on one site the already clean tone is kept.
- corner radius (decoration, 4 sites): On all four sites the AI-built pages changed corner radii, typically moving from square or small radii to rounder corners (small, medium, large or pill).
- visual effects (decoration, 4 sites): Visual effects changed on all four sites, most often with shadows replaced by thin borders or flatter styling and fewer transitions, though one site gained gradients, blur and more transitions.
- page layout (layout, 4 sites): Most AI-built pages replaced two-column sidebar or feed layouts with a single centered or stacked full-width section flow, while others moved to flex-plus-grid structures, narrower centered columns or half-width text columns.
- page length (layout, 4 sites): Overall page height changed substantially on most sites, with equal numbers becoming much taller or much shorter, and one empty gap above the footer removed.
- dom structure (semantic_dom, 4 sites): Most AI-built pages replace heavy, div- or plugin-laden markup with minimal markup and far fewer class names, with isolated changes to entry-list and card link markup.
- heading hierarchy (semantic_dom, 4 sites): All four AI-built pages turn a flat or irregular heading outline into a structured h1-h3 or h1-h4 hierarchy, and one drops headings used for menu labels.
- landmark elements (semantic_dom, 4 sites): All four AI-built pages change their landmark usage, generally consolidating repeated headers, asides and footers into a single header with fewer sections or introducing semantic landmarks in place of generic divs.
- page metadata (semantic_dom, 4 sites): All four AI-built pages drop rich social and JSON-LD metadata in favor of minimal meta tags, and some also rewrite the page title.
- image alt text (semantic_dom, 4 sites): Three of four AI-built pages replace missing, empty or wrong alt text with descriptive alts, while one keeps its existing mix of empty editorial and descriptive logo alts.
- body text size and line height (typography, 4 sites): On all four sites body text was changed to a slightly larger size (16–17px) with a line height of about 1.6–1.65.
- body typeface (typography, 4 sites): All four sites had their original body typefaces replaced, most often by a system-ui stack or a different sans/serif pairing.
- background colors (color, 3 sites): Where background treatment is reported, the AI-built pages replaced the original grounds with different ones, using alternating cream, peach or dark section bands or an off-white page ground.
- sidebar widgets (relocated or dropped) (component, 3 sites): Sidebar widgets were mostly moved out of the sidebar into headers, hero areas, full-width sections or the footer, and a few were dropped entirely.
