# The Trendiest Day Club in
Scottsdale, AZ

Looking for a unique location for your next day or night out? The Maya Day Club is the best day club, night club, and all around party space for every occasion. With great drinks and delicious food served at our outdoor beer garden and restaurant, we are a cut above the other clubs in Scottsdale, AZ.

## Making Great Memories at a Great Venue

At our clubhouse, we are dedicated to keeping the fun and games going at all hours of the day. Our 20,000 square foot outdoor venue is designed with backyard parties and tropical fun in mind, letting you enjoy beautiful sunshine and warm summer nights alike.

Further capturing the house party experience, we provide popular games such as skeeball, giant pong and connect four. We even have an on-site outdoor pool for you to enjoy. All of this lets you keep the party going after you have finished enjoying our chicken wings, burgers, fries, beers, and much more. We even offer brunch on weekend mornings. Either way, we are the ideal spot for celebrating milestones or just having a night out with your friends.

For great memories and great times, look no further than Maya Day Club in Scottsdale, AZ. We are the best pool party club, and night and day club in the Greater Phoenix Area, and are always open for reservations or private events. All you have to do is contact us today.
