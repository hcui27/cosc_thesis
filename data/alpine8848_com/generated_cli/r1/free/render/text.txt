Welcome to Alpine Everest.

Alpine Everest Expedition and Treks was founded to give its guests the best possible service. Our business motto is guest satisfaction on every tour and trek, delivered under our close supervision. We believe strongly in quality service, and our main aim is to make each customer’s trip smooth and enjoyable.

Because we value personal service, we build custom-tailored itineraries so travellers are not forced onto fixed programmes. In the coming years we plan to offer many holiday package tours, including high-adventure and soft-adventure trips, school trips and family holidays. Remember us for reliability, quality service and stylish tours — we are always at your service.
