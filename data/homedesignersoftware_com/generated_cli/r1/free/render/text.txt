Features of the software
Roofs, foundations & framing

Advanced tools build the structure for you. Drawing walls generates a 3D model automatically. Placing a door or window cuts it into the wall and framing updates automatically. Moving walls updates the roof and foundation as well.

Materials list & budgeting

Everything you add to a design goes into a materials list, allowing cost estimates and budgeting.

Kitchen & bath remodeling

Cabinets are smart objects that snap, bump and conform to walls, available in many types. A Material Painter lets you switch colors and styles quickly to compare options.

Renovation projects

Updating a master bath, modernizing an interior or building a deck? A space planning assistant helps lay out floor plans, and the 3D Library holds thousands of paints, materials and furnishings.

Work in 2D and 3D

2D floor plans show room layout and dimensions. 3D offers realistic visualization, including virtual tours, so you can experience a design before it is built.
