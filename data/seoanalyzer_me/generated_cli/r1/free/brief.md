## Seo Analyzer

Seo Analyzer is an online tool, available at https://seoanalyzer.me/, that checks websites for SEO problems. The interface offers a language option (currently EN). There are two main pages: Home (https://seoanalyzer.me/) and Site vs Site (https://seoanalyzer.me/site-vs-site).

## Core message

- Tagline: get an instant analysis of your SEO problems.
- Supporting line: the tool pinpoints SEO errors and helps you improve your site's content for better optimization.
- A "Review" label appears alongside example analysed sites.

## Features

- **Unlimited Analysis:** Run as many analyses as you want, processed on the service's most powerful servers. Saved reports let you look back at progress and earlier work.
- **In-Depth Reviews:** Detailed website analysis that explains how to fix SEO issues, with a plain definition for every SEO metric.
- **Competitive Analysis:** Compare your site's SEO side by side with competitors to see where you can improve against them.
