Welcome to Grant Aviation

Our long-running “Fly Easy, Fly Grant” policy continues: no change fees and no cancellation fees.

COVID-19 travel updates
Following CDC guidance on face coverings, guests must wear one starting May 20, 2020.
Guests bring their own coverings and must wear them in the airport and for the whole flight.
Staff who cannot keep proper social distance while working, including pilots and customer service agents, must also wear masks from May 20, 2020.
Please make sure you meet all entry requirements of the communities you are travelling to. Many communities currently require signed approval forms from local authorities.
