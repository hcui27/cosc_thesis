COVID-19 Travel Updates - We continue our long standing "Fly Easy, Fly Grant" no change no cancellation fee policy. In response to CDC guidelines regarding the use of face coverings, we are requiring guests to wear facial coverings beginning May 20, 2020. Guests will be expected to bring their own coverings and will be required to wear them while in the airport and throughout their flight. Grant Aviation employees who are unable to maintain appropriate social distance while performing their duties will also be require to wear masks beginning May 20, 2020 including pilots and customer service agents. Please ensure you meet all travel requirements for the communities you are booking travel to, many communities require signed approval forms from the local authorities at this time.

The Grant workplace is both fun and challenging, and provides opportunities for staff to develop new skills and advance in their careers. If you have a can-do attitude and a commitment to professionalism, Grant is the place for you. Join Us!

Alaska Charter Flights

In addition to our scheduled flights, Grant Aviation offers charter flight services to meet your specific needs. A charter includes the entire aircraft and your pilot for the amount of time necessary to reach your desired destination. Charters can be an economical option if you are travelling with a large group (6-9 passengers).
