## Flight search and booking
A flight search form lets visitors choose between a return trip and a one-way trip, and enter:
- Departure location
- Destination
- Departure date
- Return date
- Number of adults, children and infants

A button runs the search. Online booking is at https://flygrant.tflite.com/Public/GRT/Booking/Search. Travel agents have their own login at https://flygrant.tflite.com/Public/GRT/Account/Login. Phone reservations are available at 888-Fly-Grant or 888-359-4726.

The flight schedule and flight status can be checked at https://flygrant.tflite.com/Public/GRT/FlightInfo/FlightStatus.

## Welcome and COVID-19 travel updates
Visitors are welcomed to Grant Aviation.

COVID-19 notice:
- The long-running "Fly Easy, Fly Grant" policy continues: no change fees and no cancellation fees.
- Following CDC guidance on face coverings, guests must wear one starting May 20, 2020.
- Guests bring their own coverings and must wear them in the airport and for the whole flight.
- Staff who cannot keep proper social distance while working, including pilots and customer service agents, must also wear masks from May 20, 2020.
- Travellers must make sure they meet all entry requirements of the communities they are travelling to. Many communities currently require signed approval forms from local authorities.

## Alaska charter flights
Besides regular scheduled service, Grant Aviation provides charters tailored to individual needs. A charter covers the whole aircraft plus a pilot for as long as needed to reach the chosen destination. It can be a cost-effective choice for large groups of 6-9 passengers. More details: https://www.flygrant.com/flying-with-us/charters-groups/
