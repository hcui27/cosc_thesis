About Thync

Five questions, answered

What does Thync do?

We make devices anyone can use that improve brain function through electrical stimulation. Over the years we have researched how to open up neurostimulation to the general public.

How does electricity improve brain function?

For more than 10 years we have developed and tested non-invasive neurostimulation devices, including headsets and patches. The wearables deliver stimulation programs we designed. These programs target chosen regions of the brain and peripheral nervous system to produce the intended positive effect.

Why do we believe non-invasive brain stimulation works?

Two reasons. First, the team compared its work against the wider body of scientific literature. Second, we have tested many versions of our products for over ten years, in research labs, with respected partner institutions and influential people, and on team members ourselves.

How is the technology validated?

An in-house product research team continually runs trials on how electric stimulation affects health, behavior and physiology. We also work with academic labs and organisations worldwide to test products on distinctive groups: athletes, gamers, military personnel and surgeons.
