## Organisation overview
Thync Global, Inc. (© 2021) builds wearable devices that use electricity, which it describes as the brain's natural language, to improve brain function. The company was founded in 2010. Its mission is to make the benefits of neurostimulation available to everyone.

## Newest product: FeelZing Energy Patch
A new innovation, the FeelZing Energy Patch, is a wearable patch. It boosts energy, clarity and alertness for a few hours, much like a "5-hour energy" drink, but delivered by neurostimulation. Call to action: "Discover Feelzing", linking to https://www.feelzing.com/. Visitors can also learn about and try the technology at feelzing.com (http://feelzing.com).

## Five questions and answers about Thync
1. **What Thync does:** It makes devices anyone can use that improve brain function through electrical stimulation. Over the years it has researched how to open up neurostimulation to the general public.
2. **How electricity improves brain function:** For more than 10 years the company has developed and tested non-invasive neurostimulation devices, including headsets and patches. The wearables deliver stimulation programs the company designed. These programs target chosen regions of the brain and peripheral nervous system to produce the intended positive effect.
3. **Why non-invasive brain stimulation is believed to work:** There are two reasons. First, the team compared its work against the wider body of scientific literature. Second, it has tested many versions of its products for over ten years. This testing took place in research labs, with respected partner institutions and influential people, and on the team members themselves.
4. **How the technology is validated:** An in-house product research team continually runs trials on how electric stimulation affects health, behavior and physiology. Thync also works with academic labs and organisations worldwide to test products on distinctive groups: athletes, gamers, military personnel and surgeons.
5. **How to try the technology:** Go to feelzing.com to find the wearable patch described above.
