## Organisation overview
Gravity Vault is a chain of indoor rock climbing gyms and describes itself as the first indoor rock climbing franchise. ![](images/logo.png)

Mission statement: the gyms aim to make every visit a positive, memorable climbing experience. They work to be welcoming, diverse, equitable and inclusive to customers and staff alike. Young or old, big or small, beginner or expert, everyone should feel the gym is a second home. The company believes new heights can be reached on the walls and in life, and that people get there best together.
