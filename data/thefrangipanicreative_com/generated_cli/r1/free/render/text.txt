ABOUT
Meet Corinne Rodrigues

The Frangipani Creative is the studio run by Corinne Rodrigues, based in Secunderabad, India. It offers training and coaching in writing, blogging and creativity.

Corinne is a woman enjoying midlife, who has moved from self-improvement toward self-acceptance and being happy as herself.

Details of programs, courses, training and other offline events are coming soon.
A lover of words, a blogger on self-acceptance, and a book reviewer.
Writes about life, wellness and relationships at Everyday Gyaan.
An avid reader who reviews books at CorinneRodrigues.com.
Offers coaching at The Frangipani Creative for anyone wanting to explore their creativity.
